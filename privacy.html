YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy by design.

Health and contact information deserves careful handling. These controls outline how a real CareCamp service should protect participant data.

♧

Collect only what's needed

Ask for the minimum information required to register participants, assess eligibility and coordinate appointments.

⌑

Limit access to authorized staff

Use individual accounts and role-based permissions. Organizers should only see information needed for their camp responsibilities.

⟲

Set a retention period

Define how long registration records are kept, then securely delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

▤

Use aggregated reports

Show totals and trends rather than participant names or personal health details in reports.

PARTICIPANT CHECKLIST

Know your choices.

Before submitting a registration, participants should be able to:

  • Read why their information is being collected.
  • Understand who can access it.
  • Choose whether to receive optional reminders.
  • Ask the organizer to correct inaccurate details.
  • Contact the responsible organization with privacy questions.

Important implementation note

This project is a front-end demonstration, not a secure medical records system. Do not enter real patient or sensitive health information. Before real-world deployment, add secure authentication, encrypted storage and transport, access logs, consent management, retention/deletion workflows and a privacy review aligned with applicable law.
