Help & Library Rules

Everything you need to know about using our library.

📖 How to Book a Seat?

  1. Open the Library Seat Map.
  2. Select an available green seat.
  3. Enter your student details.
  4. Select your booking date and time.
  5. Click Confirm Booking.
  6. Check your Booking History.

📚 Library Rules

  • Maintain silence inside the library.
  • Keep your student ID with you.
  • Do not disturb other students.
  • Keep your study area clean.
  • Take care of library resources.
  • Follow library opening hours.

💻 Technical Support

Facing problems while booking your seat? Contact the library administration for assistance.
